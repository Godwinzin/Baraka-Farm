Cabbage Production

Usually covers around 2 acres of the farm per season.

  • Growth Duration: 70-120 days depending on variety.
  • Varieties: We mainly plant Gloria F1, Copenhagen Market, and Pruktor F1.
  • Soil Type: Rich, well-drained loam (pH 6.0 - 6.5).
  • Spacing: 60 cm between rows, 45 cm between plants.
  • Fertilizer: DAP at planting; CAN and foliar feeds later during growth.
  • Pests & Diseases: Aphids, black rot.
  • Yield: 15,000 - 30,000 heads per acre.
