Privacy Notice

When you request a visit, we collect your name, phone number, preferred visit date and, if you choose to give it, your email address.

We use these details only to arrange your visit and to contact you about it.

Your details are stored in a database and can be seen only by the farm owner, through a password-protected page. The site is run on services provided by Vercel, Render and Neon, and a notification email is sent to the owner through Resend. We do not sell your details or share them for advertising.

We keep your details only as long as needed to arrange your visit. To ask us to correct or delete them, contact barakafarm@gmail.com.
